Private Events — Corporate Events

When the stakes are high, so are the standards.

Corporate events at Canlis are designed for the kinds of evenings where relationships deepen, partnerships begin, and important conversations deserve more than a private conference room.

Clients, investors, leadership teams, and creative partners gather here because the setting changes the tone of the night. The city glowing below. A beautifully set table. Service that feels attentive without interruption. An atmosphere that allows people to settle in, speak openly, and actually enjoy being together.

Explore our private rooms

The Penthouse

Capacity: 25–110

Originally built as our grandfather’s residence, the Penthouse sits above our dining room and is approximately 1,500 square feet. It features a full-service bar, dining-room views, wrap-around views of Lake Union and the Cascade Mountains, and a baby grand piano.

Explore the room

The Executive Room

Capacity: 6–24

Over 400 square feet of warmth and comfort. This private space is on our main level and features a fireplace and North and East views of Lake Union and the Cascade Mountains through 10-foot-high windows.
